Our Story

TechFix Pro was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to provide fast, affordable, and reliable tech repair services without the runaround you get from big box stores.

What started as a one-person operation fixing smartphones has grown into a full-service technology solutions company serving both residential and commercial clients across Ibadan.

Unlike our competitors, we don't believe in pushing unnecessary services or parts. We diagnose the problem, explain your options in plain language, and get your devices working again as quickly as possible.
