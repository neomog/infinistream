Privacy Policy

How we protect and use your information

Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, email, phone number, and address for service coordination
  • Device Information: Make, model, serial numbers, and repair history
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through our payment partners (we don't store full credit card details)
  • Website Usage Data: Through cookies and analytics to improve our services

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our repair and installation services
  • Process transactions and send service updates
  • Respond to customer service requests
  • Send promotional communications (you can opt-out anytime)
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure payment processing
  • Limited employee access to personal information
  • Regular security audits

Third-Party Sharing

We only share information when necessary for:

  • Payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Warranty claims with manufacturers
  • Legal compliance or protection of rights

We never sell customer data to third parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The updated version will be posted on our website with a new effective date.
